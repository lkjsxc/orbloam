 A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Watch. Wander. Let life grow.

Fifteen little minutes

Residents gather and work by themselves. A life lasts 15 minutes, then returns Essence to your core and begins a new generation. Research and buildings remain. Your first 16 places fill gradually; Vitality discoveries add 48 more.

Your world, not a dashboard

Click a core, resident, resource, or workshop for details. Click empty ground to move your core. Drag to pan and scroll or pinch to zoom. In the distant atlas, click a region to see its resources or double-click to look closer. Home returns to your core; Escape closes a panel. H hides the interface.

A tree, not a checklist

Each family forks into separate paths. A discovery requires its connected parent, not every earlier number. Every discovery strengthens that family. Nothing is lost when you choose another branch later.

Workshops think for themselves

Build a workshop once. It chooses unlocked, affordable recipes according to the stock you need, finishes work in progress, and maintains itself while near your core and supplied with workers, timber, and stone. Open a workshop only to override its recipe, pause it, repair it immediately, or dismantle it.

The wider landscape

Deposits are finite and shared. They regrow over time. Moving and researching Reach open richer gathering areas. At a distance, the atlas combines deposits into true regional totals; it does not change the simulation. Walking and carrying are visual representations, not individual pathfinding.

Away and back again

The native server resolves elapsed 10-second steps in order when someone connects. After a long absence, catch-up can delay actions. An uncertain action is retried with the same ID, never silently duplicated. Keep both your recovery key and the server’s data backup.
